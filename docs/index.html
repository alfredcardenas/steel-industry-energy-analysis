HOW TO READ THIS VIEW

r is the Pearson correlation computed with CORR() across all 35,040 intervals; R² (= r²) is the share of variance a linear fit explains. The two charts plot load-type averages, not individual intervals. Linear fits on the same intervals as the Tableau scatter plots give R² of 0.70–0.89 for reactive power (one line per load type, as in Tableau) and 0.98 for CO₂ (one line for all intervals). These are associations, not causes: reactive power and CO₂ move closely with consumption, while power factor is only moderately related.
